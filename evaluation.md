Evaluation of AI-Generated Media
Artifact 1: ElevenLabs (AI Voice)

Purpose: Generate an AI voice narration from the Task 5 coaching recommendation.

Strengths
Voice sounded natural and clear.
Pronunciation of most words was accurate.
Generation was quick and easy.
Audio quality was high with very little background noise.
Weaknesses
Voice had limited emotion and sounded slightly monotone.
The free plan had a character limit, so I had to shorten my script.
Voice alone lacks facial expressions or visual context.
Realism

The audio sounded realistic enough that a listener could mistake it for a real person if they were unfamiliar with AI-generated speech.

Ease of Use

Very easy to use. The interface was straightforward, and the audio was generated within a few seconds.

Artifact 2: HeyGen (AI Avatar Video)

Purpose: Create a talking avatar using the same narration.

Strengths
Lip synchronization was generally accurate.
Facial expressions and head movements made the presentation more engaging.
The avatar looked professional.
Weaknesses
Some mouth movements were slightly unnatural.
The free version did not allow direct video download, so I had to record the preview.
Avatar expressions occasionally looked repetitive.
Realism

The video appeared convincing at first glance, but small facial movements and blinking patterns made it possible to recognize it as AI-generated during closer inspection.

Ease of Use

The interface was simple, but the free-tier limitations reduced usability compared with the voice-generation tool.

Overall Comparison
Feature	ElevenLabs	HeyGen
Ease of use	Excellent	Good
Audio quality	Excellent	Good
Visual realism	N/A	Good
Naturalness	Very High	High
Free version limitations	Character limit	No video download
Best use case	Podcasts, narration, voiceovers	Presentations, virtual spokespersons
Conclusion

Both tools successfully generated synthetic media from the same script. ElevenLabs produced more natural speech with minimal effort, while HeyGen created a more engaging presentation by combining speech with an animated avatar. However, the free versions of both platforms imposed limitations that affected the workflow. ElevenLabs restricted the number of characters that could be converted into speech, while HeyGen did not allow direct downloading of the generated video. Overall, I found ElevenLabs to be more practical for creating high-quality narration, whereas HeyGen was more effective for visual presentations despite its download restrictions.

Responsible Use

Both artifacts were created for academic experimentation and are clearly identified as AI-generated. The experiment did not attempt to impersonate a real individual. The results demonstrate why synthetic media should be transparently labeled, especially as generative tools continue to produce increasingly realistic audio and video.
